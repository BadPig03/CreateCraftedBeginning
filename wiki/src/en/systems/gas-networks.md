# Gas networks {#gas-networks}

Think of a gas system in three parts: where the gas comes from, how it travels and which device uses it.

<img class="item-icon" src="/assets/gas-canister.png" alt="The mod's gas canister item icon" width="64" height="64" />

## Start with a short route {#start-small}

Get to know canisters and airtight pipes before building a complex network. Airtight pipes provide a passive route; they do not generate pressure. Connecting a pipe does not by itself establish a working gas supply.

```text
Supply → Airtight pipe → Consuming device
```

This diagram shows roles. Check the actual ports, supply method and operating conditions of the devices you choose.

## Pipes, pumps and valves {#parts}

| Part | Start with this role |
| --- | --- |
| Gas canister | Store and carry gas |
| Airtight pipe | Connect gas devices into a transport route |
| Pumps and valves | Control transport and connections; exact capabilities depend on the device |

::: tip Keep the experiment small
Start with one gas and one short route. Check both ends and the operating conditions before adding branches.
:::
