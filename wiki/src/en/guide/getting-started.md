# Getting started {#getting-started}

Open a creative-mode test world and pick one system to experiment with. A few blocks make it easier to see how things work together than an entire factory does.

::: info Sample guide
This first set of pages is here to try navigation, search and language switching. Full installation instructions and device specifications will follow.
:::

## Pick a route {#pick-a-route}

- **Into pipes and machinery?** Start with [gas networks](../systems/gas-networks.md) to meet canisters, airtight pipes and supply devices.
- **Into beams and layouts?** Start with [amethyst optics](../systems/amethyst-optics.md) to meet mirrors and lenses.

## Use the in-game tools {#in-game-tools}

Look up recipes in JEI for your current modpack. Where an item supports Ponder, watch its in-game demonstration. Modpacks can change recipes, so use the recipes shown in your game.

Change one thing at a time: adjust a pipe connection or move a mirror, then observe the result. This makes it easier to understand what caused the change.

## Keep exploring {#keep-exploring}

Check [questions & answers](./faq.md) if you get stuck. You can also try the search at the top of the page: look for “airtight pipe” or “amethyst”.
